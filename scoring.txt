Typical play means two teams of two people each.

The game is played with six decks of cards including jokers

The game has four rounds. Each round is scored separately from all the
other rounds.

After the initial shuffle, each player cuts the deck for a card to see
who gets to go first. Highest card wins, Jokers are the highest, two
is lowest.

the first round starts with the high card player cutting off a stack
of cards to deal the 15 card hand, The next player clockwise cutting a
stack to deal the 13 foot, and the next player cutting a stack to deal
the 11 card toe.

Each of the dealers will establish four stacks of cards in front of
him or herself, each consisting of the appropriate number of
cards.This phase results in the first scoring event of a round. If a
player gets exactly the number of cards in his cut to complete his
deal, the team scores 200 points.This is only applicable to the hand
and the foot. The toe is not eligible for this bonus.

After the three players have completed their individual deals in front
of themselves, the cards, the stacks of cards are distributed to all
four players. The first stack distributed is the toe, the second is
the foot, the last is the hand.

As the round begins, each team must meet a minimum required number of
points to meld and start putting cards on the table from their
hands. the meld value is based on the team's total score at the start
of the round.


score >=     0	requires  50 points to meld
score >=  7500	requires  90 points to meld
score >= 15000	requires 120 points to meld
score >= 22500	requires 150 points to meld
score >= 30000	requires 180 points to meld
score >= 37500	requires 220 points to meld

play continues until one team "goes out"

Scoring at the end of the round consists of counting the number of
clean canastas, dirty canastas, sevens canastas, fives canastas,
wildcard canastas, and red threes.

Each clean canasta is worth 500 points. Each dirty canasta is worth
300 points. The seven's canastas are worth 2,000 points each. Five's
canastas are worth 1500 points each, and ildcard canastas are worth a
thousand points each. Red threes are worth 100 points each. In
addition, the team that goes out gets an extra 500-point bonus.Cards
held by any player after a team goes out are counted against the
player's team.

After the canastas and red threes are counted, the individual cards
are counted. Cards held in a player's hand are negative, cards on the
table are a positive.

Card values are as follows four through seven are worth five points
each, eight through king are worth 10 points each, twos and aces are
worth 20 points each, wildcards are worth 50 points each. Red threes
are a hundred points each, only as a negative if held in someone's
hand. black threes are five points each.

After the scores are calculated, the total of this round and any
previous rounds indicates the meld for the next round. The game is
over after four rounds.
